Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

SIGNAL is a co-op mystery game where squads race to solve a case together. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Display name. The name you type when you join a drop, shown to other players in that drop.
  • Anonymous device token. A random identifier stored on your device so the app can reconnect you to your drop. It is not tied to your real identity, email, or phone number.
  • Gameplay data. The answers and actions you submit during a drop, so the game can run.

What we don't do

  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No ads, and no selling or sharing of data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No collection of contacts, location, photos, or health data.

Payments

Any purchases are processed by Whop. SIGNAL never sees or stores your card details. Whop's own privacy policy applies to that data.

Storage and deletion

Game data is stored with our backend provider, Convex. To have your data deleted, email razeenali01@gmail.com with your display name and the drop code you used.
